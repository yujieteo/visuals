# Your energy dips mid-afternoon. Your inbox doesn't.

`index.html` is generated by `build.py` from `raw.json` and `meta.json`: never hand-edit it. Change those or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, then check with `python3 ../../scripts/check.py energy-email-productivity`.

Its tests are in `tests/`, imported with their history from [yujieteo/energy-email-productivity](https://github.com/yujieteo/energy-email-productivity). Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
