# Good food in Ubi, Hougang, Woodleigh, Paya Lebar and Eunos

Generated by `scripts/new_visual.py` (see `generated.json`). `dataset.py` ranks the places in `outlets.csv` with `sources.json`, `directories.csv`, `yeo2021.csv`, `fndds.csv` and `map.json`, refuses a row that breaks a rule (an unknown guide, a station more than 1 km away, an open place missing from its directory), and writes `raw.json`. `python3 dataset.py --verify` checks that `raw.json` is current. Rerun `python3 extract_fndds.py <FoodData Central survey zip>` only when `outlets.csv` names a new FNDDS food (its docstring gives the download).

After a data change, run `python3 dataset.py && python3 build.py`. The page is `src/model.js` (state fields, examples, derive, map markers), `src/view.js` (render, maps, WebMCP tools), `src/body.html`, `src/style.css` and `report.js`. Edit them, then run `python3 build.py`. Never hand-edit `index.html`.

Calorie figures are estimates for reference dishes, not measurements of a place's food. A dish without a fixed portion stays unestimated, with its reason.

The mechanical files (`build.py`, `beamdswitch.js`, `tsconfig.json`, `types/globals.d.ts`, `tests/ubi-hougang-food-kit.test.mjs`) come from the generator: `python3 ../../scripts/new_visual.py --check ubi-hougang-food` reports drift and `--update ubi-hougang-food` rewrites them. The tests are in `tests/`. `python3 ../../scripts/check.py ubi-hougang-food` runs every check. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
