Sectionlab

Build a cross-section from library shapes (drag them in, move them, resize them, round any corner, cut holes) and read its section properties, its torsion constant where a verified formula exists, and its moment–curvature curve for Ramberg–Osgood materials. Units are mm, MPa, N and N·mm.

Verify independently. These results come from this page's own calculation. They are not a design-code compliance check.

Drag a shape from the palette onto the section, or press it to add it beside the section. Drag a part to move it and its square handles to resize it; parts snap to the grid and to each other's edges (hold Alt to place freely). Click a corner dot, or pick it from the corner list, to set its radius; 0 is sharp.
