The checks


The rules follow John Doerr's Measure What Matters and Google's re:Work guide. Each links its source. The page tests only the wording it can test, and flags words as hints, not proof.


    

    Progress of a key result is (current − start) ÷ (target − start), held between 0 and 100%. A score key result sets start, target and current on the 0.0 to 1.0 scale. An objective is the mean of its key results.
