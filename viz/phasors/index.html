Phasor and Impedance Visualiser

Drive a series or parallel RLC circuit with a sine wave and watch what it does: the element voltages or branch currents add up as rotating arrows, the impedance and admittance planes show why, and the waveforms show the resulting lag or lead. Every number is on the page, so it also works as a checker.

Textbook steady-state AC with ideal components: no transients, no source impedance, no losses beyond R.
