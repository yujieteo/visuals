Frequency-Response Visualiser

See how poles, zeros, gain, delay and a controller shape a feedback loop's frequency response, and what that does to its margins and closed-loop stability. It covers single loops and multivariable (MIMO) loops in continuous or discrete time, on Bode, singular-value, Nyquist and Nichols views and a pole-zero map.

            Exploration only. Classical textbook methods (Bode, gain, phase and delay margins, sensitivity peaks, zero-order hold, Tustin and Euler discretisation) with a hand-written root finder and matrix exponential; no library, no network. MIMO loops use a hand-written complex linear-algebra kernel (Hessenberg-QR eigenvalues, Jacobi SVD, LU); disk margins and μ analysis are out of scope.
