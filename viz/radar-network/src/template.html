Radar network: the range equation in 3D

Three moving radars and four moving targets give 36 links, one for each transmitter, receiver and target. Each link shows its radar equation, numbers, SNR, detector threshold, required SNR and margin. Move the objects or the time and see which factor changes the result.

All absolute values are synthetic: power, gains, losses, RCS and the clutter patches. Only the carriers (4, 10 and 17 GHz) come from the supplied evidence. The NASA F-117 model curves stay in a separate evidence panel. Their magnitude reference is not stated, so they never enter a power or range result.

3D scene

Controls

Every value in the scene has a numeric control here. Edits pause time, enter the undo history with their time, and recalculate the analytic results at once. Sampled results then show “Result from previous parameters” until you recalculate.
