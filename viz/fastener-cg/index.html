Fastener Pattern CG Tracker

Place a fastener group on the canvas or in the table. The page reports its shear, axial and area centroids, polar moment J, Ixx, Iyy, Ixy and principal axes, reduces a general 3D load applied anywhere to the group, and shares it among the fasteners by the elastic method. Key in shear and tension allowables to get each fastener's exact load-scale-factor margin of safety under a shear-tension interaction, and the critical fastener. Every number is recalculated as you edit.

Preliminary sizing — verify against the governing specification. The tool ships no allowables and follows no single design code.

    Sign convention

    • Right-handed: x right, y up, z toward you.
    • Faying surface z = 0; fasteners at (x, y).
    • +Fz is tension (pulls the loaded plate away).
    • Moments by the right-hand rule; +Mz is CCW.

    Methods

    Fasteners on the far side of the edge carry T = M_L·ka·d/Σ(ka·d²); the rest carry none. v1 needs zero moment about the axis perpendicular to the edge (E-012).

    Group defaults

    Fasteners use these unless a table cell overrides them (bold). Clearing a cell returns it to the default. The tool ships no allowables: a check without one is not evaluated.

    Shear-tension interaction

    IF(k) = (k·Rs / Fs)a + (k·Rt / Ft)b; MS = k* − 1 where IF(k*) = 1.

    MS is the exact multiplier on the whole applied load at which IF reaches 1. With (a, b) ≠ (1, 1) it differs from 1/IF − 1 (W-006); the plain IF(1) is shown beside it.

    ICR method

    In-plane shear only (Fx, Fy, Mz,s); ks, tension, prying and preload never enter. The governing fastener (smallest Δmax/ρ) reaches Δmax; the others deform in proportion to their distance from the ICR.

    μ = 0.3937/mm, λ = 0.55 and Δmax = 8.6 mm are the metric equivalents of the common structural-bolt curve: editable defaults, not recommendations (N-008). Choose the ICR design basis under Methods to run the checks on ICR reactions.

    Prying and preload

    b' = b − D/2, a' = min(a, 1.25b) + D/2, ρ = b'/a', δ = 1 − d_h/p, t_c = √(4Bb'/(pFp)), α' = (1/δ)[(T/B)(t_c/t)² − 1] ∈ [0, 1], Q = Bδα'ρ(t/t_c)²

    Flange thickness t and flange strength Fp come from the loaded plate (Plates card). A manual factor f makes the bolt tension f·T (W-012).

    F_b = max(P_max + φT, T) + Q; clamp = P_min − (1 − φ)T; separation at T = P_min/(1 − φ)

    Torque calculator, T = K·D·P

    φ is required and has no default. Friction slip is not evaluated (N-005).

    Plates

    Up to two plates, as axis-aligned rectangles. Every fastener passes through every plate. Fbr (bearing stress) or a direct bearing allowable, Fsu (shear-out stress) and the minimum e/D are keyed per plate; a blank allowable leaves that check not evaluated. The loaded plate's thickness and Fp feed prying.

      The published-reference cases use openly readable sources, not the current AISC Manual: VR-02 compares ICR coefficients with Table 13.1 of the Kulak, Fisher and Struik Guide to Design Criteria for Bolted and Riveted Joints, 2nd ed.       Method

      • Three centroids, always reported separately: shear Cs (weighted by ks; the headline CG), axial Ca (weighted by ka) and area Cg (weighted by A).
      • J = Σ ks(u² + v²) about Cs. Ixx = Σ ka q², Iyy = Σ ka p², Ixy = Σ ka p q about Ca. Principal values I₁,₂ = (Ixx + Iyy)/2 ± √(((Ixx − Iyy)/2)² + Ixy²); θp = ½ atan2(−2Ixy, Ixx − Iyy), CCW from +x to the I₁ axis. Section properties are in length² × weight.
      • Load reduction from P = (xp, yp, zp) to Q on z = 0 with r = P − Q: Mx,Q = Mx + ry Fz − zp Fy; My,Q = My + zp Fx − rx Fz; Mz,Q = Mz + rx Fy − ry Fx. Shear and torsion reduce to Cs, axial load and bending to Ca.
      • Elastic in-plane shear: direct Rd = F ks/Σks; torsional Rtx = −Mz,s ks v / J, Rty = +Mz,s ks u / J.
      • Axial method (a), rigid plate with the neutral axis through Ca: D = Ixx Iyy − Ixy², θx = (Iyy Mx,a + Ixy My,a)/D, θy = (Ixy Mx,a + Ixx My,a)/D, T = ka(Fz/Ka + θx q − θy p). Negative T is unloading (clamp-up), shown as computed.
      • Interaction: IF(k) = (k Rs/Fs)^a + (k Rt/Ft)^b with separate exponents a and b (presets: elliptical (2, 2), the default, and linear (1, 1)). Rt is the positive tension; unloading counts as zero (N-006). The margin is the exact load scale factor, MS = k* − 1 with IF(k*) = 1, found by a bracketed Brent search (tolerance 1e-12 on k, 200 iterations); a failed solve shows “MS not computed” (W-008) and never a substitute number. For a = b = 1 it equals 1/IF − 1. The governing MS of a fastener is its lowest margin across modes, and the critical fastener has the lowest governing MS.
      • Prying (T-stub, keyed B and Fp, no code factor built in) applies only to positive external tension T: a is limited to 1.25·b; b' = b − D/2, a' = a + D/2, ρ = b'/a', δ = 1 − d_h/p, t_c = √(4Bb'/(pFp)), α' = (1/δ)[(T/B)(t_c/t)² − 1] clamped to [0, 1], Q = Bδα'ρ(t/t_c)². t and Fp are the loaded plate's thickness and flange strength. A manual amplification factor f replaces the T-stub with a bolt tension f·T (W-012).
      • Preload P_max, P_min and load-sharing factor φ (required, 0 < φ < 1): F_b = max(P_max + φT, T) + Q, clamp force P_min − (1 − φ)T, separation load P_min/(1 − φ). Once clamp force reaches zero the joint is separated (W-013). F_b replaces the tension in the interaction and is re-evaluated at every load multiplier k; preload itself does not scale. Clamp force is reported but no friction-slip capacity is claimed (N-005). With preload off the tension is T + Q.
      • Plates (up to two, axis-aligned rectangles): every fastener is checked against every plate and the governing plate is reported. Bearing R_br,allow = Fbr·D·t (or a keyed direct-load allowable), MS = R_br,allow/Rs − 1. Tear-out casts a ray from the fastener along its bearing direction to the plate edge, giving e; capacity = 2·t·(e − D/2)·Fsu and MS = capacity/Rs − 1; e/D below the keyed minimum is W-011. The bearing direction is −R in the loaded plate (it is pushed along the load, so the fastener bears on the trailing side of the hole) and +R in the other plate. Geometry checks: a fastener outside a plate (E-004), fasteners closer than their diameter (E-005), edge distance below D/2 (E-006), and a load point outside the loaded plate (W-009).
      • Axial method (b), contact edge: the selected plate edge L is the neutral axis and fasteners act in tension only. With d the distance from L on the tension side, T = M_L·ka·d/Σ(ka·d²), where M_L is the moment about L (reduced to the projection of Ca onto L, so it includes the Fz lever) that puts the far side in tension. The contact reaction is C = ΣT − Fz. M_L ≤ 0 gives no tension (W-010: wrong edge), and C < 0 means no contact (W-021: use method (a)). In v1 the moment about the axis perpendicular to L must be zero (E-012).
      • ICR method (in-plane shear only; ks, tension, prying, preload and Fz never enter). Crawford-Kulak R = Rult(1 − e^(−μΔ))^λ or elastic-perfectly-plastic R = Rult·min(Δ/Δy, 1) with fracture at Δmax. Deformation is proportional to the distance ρ from the ICR, Δᵢ = Δmax,gov·ρᵢ/ρ_gov, where the governing fastener has the smallest Δmax/ρ. The search is anchored on the Rult-weighted centroid of the fasteners, which does not depend on ks (it equals Cs for uniform ks and Rult). The ICR is searched on the line through that point perpendicular to the load direction, at a distance from the load's line of action on the side that gives the rotation sense (both sides of the reference point are scanned), where moment and force equilibrium give the same ultimate load (bracketed Brent search, normalised moment residual 1e-6, 200 iterations). For an asymmetric group whose force perpendicular to the load does not vanish on that line, the ICR is refined off it by a damped Newton iteration so all three equilibrium equations hold. A pure moment starts at the reference point; a load is a uniform translation only when the uniform translation loads also balance its moment, and a zero in-plane load gives zero ICR shear. γ_ult = P_u/|F| scales the whole load with its eccentricity kept; γ_ult − 1 is an ultimate capacity, not comparable to the allowable-based MS. The reactions at the applied load are the ultimate reactions divided by γ_ult (proportional scaling convention, W-015). A search that does not converge shows "not converged" with its residual and no partial numbers (W-014), and the elastic result remains.
      • Design basis: elastic (default) or ICR selects which in-plane shear feeds the interaction, bearing and tear-out checks. The tension side always comes from the elastic or contact-edge distribution.
      • Equilibrium closure (ΣR = F, ΣM = M for shear and axial) is checked on every calculation to a 1e-9 relative residual; a failure blocks the results.
      • Unit switching converts stored values in place to 12 significant figures (1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 lbf = 4.4482216152605 N). Imports keep the file's unit system.
