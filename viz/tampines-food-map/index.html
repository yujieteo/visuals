How this was made

Candidates are outlets featured in at least one of 8 food guides published or updated in 2025-26 that sell mains, are listed on Google Maps inside Tampines Mall, Century Square or Tampines 1, and are not marked closed (33 qualified). They are ranked by Google Maps star rating, then by how many guides feature them, then by name; the top 20 are shown. Requiring a guide pick keeps unreviewed listings with a handful of 5-star ratings out of the ranking.

Each main is matched to a dish in HPB's Singapore Food Insights Database (SGFoodID, formerly FOCOS), which gives lab-analysed or derived energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat for one default serving. Figures are estimates for that typical serving, not the outlet's own recipe; 'closest HPB dish' marks a substitute where SGFoodID has no exact dish. The bar shows the share of energy from protein and carbohydrate (4 kcal/g) and fat (9 kcal/g). Trace values count as zero.

Limitations: Google Maps showed star ratings but withheld review counts in the logged-out view, so the ranking cannot weight by review volume. Review summaries paraphrase the cited guides, not Google reviews. Calorie figures are estimates from standard HPB servings; shared dishes (TANYU's fish, Haidilao's hot pot) are shown per person. The map places each mall's picks in a floor directory beside the mall; dots are not exact shop positions.

Guide picks left out: Tokyo Shokudo (Tampines Mall): permanently closed; Kanada-Ya (Century Square): permanently closed; S.O.S Chicken (Tampines 1): permanently closed; San.wich (Tampines 1): permanently closed; Fatburger & Buffalo's (Century Square): not found on Google Maps; Kenny Rogers Roasters (Century Square): unit now listed as XW Plus Western Grill; Mediumrare by Saveur (Century Square): unit now listed as Abundance; Aburi-EN (Tampines 1): unit now listed as Bari Bari Steak.
