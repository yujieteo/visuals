# How English Grammar Works

`index.html` is generated by `build.py` from `raw.json` (the CGEL chapter and section outline), `concepts.json`, `examples.json` (bracketed trees and contrasts), `meta.json` and `src/` (`english-grammar-logic.js`, the pure logic, and `english-grammar.js`, the interface, both typed with JSDoc that the builder strips; `english-grammar.css`): never hand-edit it. Change those or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, then check with `python3 ../../scripts/check.py english-grammar`.

The builder validates the whole corpus, one stage per part (chapters, concepts, examples, contrasts, route), each naming what is wrong; `tests/test_english_grammar.py` checks every rejection. The page must stay under 1 MB; `--verify` checks it.

Its tests are in `tests/`, imported with their history from [yujieteo/english-grammar](https://github.com/yujieteo/english-grammar). Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
