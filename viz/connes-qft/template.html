Quantum Electrodynamics

This laboratory computes everything in the browser and needs JavaScript. The three flagship computations it steps through are: the one-loop QED vacuum polarization carried from momentum integral to Laurent series, counterterm, running coupling, Hopf-algebra element and Birkhoff factorization; a spectral triple whose inner fluctuation produces a U(1) gauge field and its spectral action; and a spacetime region whose local algebra and vacuum give the Tomita operator S = JΔ1/2 and modular flow.
