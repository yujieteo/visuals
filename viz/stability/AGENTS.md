# Structural Stability Visualiser

`index.html` is generated by `build.py`, which inlines `raw.json`, `engine.js` (the pure calculation core, `Stability`; no DOM access) and `beamdswitch.js` (the site's template, unchanged) into `template.html`: never hand-edit it. `tests/test_stability.py` fails when it is stale.

Every formula carries a source tag (`NASA`, `classical` or `fit`); a chart relation outside its figure shows "unavailable", never an extrapolated number. Keep the "Not for certification" banner and its repeat in every export. Values are stored in N, mm and MPa whatever units are displayed.

Its tests are in `tests/`; `python3 ../../scripts/check.py stability` runs its checks. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
