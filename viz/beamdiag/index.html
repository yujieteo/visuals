Beam diagram creator

Build a straight beam, place pinned and fixed supports and loads, and read its reactions, shear force, bending moment and deflection. Statically indeterminate beams such as fixed–fixed spans are solved by the stiffness method. Everything is calculated on this page, and the same model downloads as a NASTRAN deck.

Ends

Drag a support or a load along the beam, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for finer steps). Move over the diagrams, or focus them and use the arrow keys, to read values; Page Up and Page Down jump between supports and loads.

      The image and the PDF hold the beam, shear force, bending moment and deflection diagrams with every result below them. They are drawn on this page and saved straight to your device: nothing is uploaded.

      The beamdswitch button saves this beam as a narrated talk: a Markdown deck with the set-up, method, results, hand calculations and checks, every number as shown here, and a spoken narration on every slide. Open it in beamdswitch to get slides, a handout, narration and a video. Copy deck puts the same deck on the clipboard to paste into beamdswitch, for when the browser blocks the download.

          Hand calculations

          The same beam worked by hand, step by step, for every segment between consecutive supports and load points and at the point selected on the diagrams: reactions from equilibrium (or from compatibility when the beam is statically indeterminate), shear and moment by the method of sections, slope and deflection by double integration with the boundary and continuity conditions, then the bending stress and an equilibrium check. The stiffness solver is authoritative: every number below is its value, written as this page writes it, and the hand steps are a readable derivation of that same answer.
