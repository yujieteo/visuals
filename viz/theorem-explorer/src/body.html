A catalog of named mathematical results from mathlib4, Wikidata, Wikipedia, nLab and the TheoremSearch dataset, and a catalog of the mathematical concepts they are stated about, from nLab, mathlib and the results themselves. Each result and each concept has rubric scores with their evidence. Prerequisite trees join results to concepts, and arXiv popularity is shown per tag: category, archive or group. Use it to find what to learn next. The file works offline and never refreshes its sources.

Decoding the catalog. This takes a few seconds on a slow device.
