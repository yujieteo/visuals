A catalog of named mathematical results from mathlib4, Wikidata, Wikipedia, nLab and the TheoremSearch dataset. Each result has rubric scores with their evidence, Lean measurements, prerequisite paths, concrete comparisons and field shares over time. Use it to find the result to learn next. The file works offline and never refreshes its sources.

      Scores are 0 to 4 per component. u: unknown, na: not applicable. A weighted u or na makes the aggregate unknown; the interval shows its range with 0 and 4 in place of the missing scores.       Formal library: every mathlib theorem

      The pinned mathlib build holds these theorems, named and unnamed, with technical lemmas. They are measured, not scored: no judge assessed them, so they have no rubric score. A linked theorem is the formal statement of a catalog result. This list decodes only when you open it.
