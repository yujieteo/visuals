# What Manchester City’s charges and accounts do and do not show

`index.html` is generated by `build.py` from `raw.csv` and `meta.json`: never hand-edit it. Change those or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, then check with `python3 ../../scripts/check.py manchester-city-finances`.

Its tests are in `tests/`, imported with their history from [yujieteo/manchester-city-finances](https://github.com/yujieteo/manchester-city-finances). Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
