# How Singapore attractions are marketed

`index.html` is generated by `build.py` from `raw.json`, `meta.json` and d3 in `vendor/`: never hand-edit it. Change those or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, then check with `python3 ../../scripts/check.py tourist-attractions`.

Its tests are in `tests/`, imported with their history from yujieteo/tourist-attractions (now deleted) into yujieteo/visuals. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
