Justin Tang's Worlds team, one turn at a time

Justin Tang finished 14th of 395 at the 2026 World Championships with Sneasler, Sinistcha, Kingambit, Mega Blastoise, Mega Delphox and Incineroar. His Round 11 feature match was a 0-2 loss to João Felipe Leite, who went on to the semifinals. This lab puts the two real team sheets against each other. For any position it lists every legal plan for both players, simulates each pairing several times with fresh damage rolls, and finds the mix of plans a strong player would use. Then you play the turn out.
