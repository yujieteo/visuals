# Win the turn: Protect, Fake Out and pivots

`index.html` is generated by `build.py`, which inlines `raw.json`, `sprites.json` and `engine.js` (the turn engine and matrix-game solver) into `template.html`: never hand-edit it. `tests/test_vgc_turn_lab.py` fails when it is stale.

Keep the engine pure (no DOM access) so Node can load it, and list anything it does not model in `raw.json`'s `not_modelled`. The page has no beamdswitch deck.

Its tests are in `tests/`; `python3 ../../scripts/check.py vgc-protect-fakeout-pivot-trainer` runs its checks. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
