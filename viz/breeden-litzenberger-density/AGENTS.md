# The risk-neutral density is the curvature of the call-price curve

`index.html` is generated by `build.py` from `raw.json`, `meta.json`, `beamdswitch.js` and `report.js`: never hand-edit it. Change those or `build.py`, then run `python3 build.py`. `build.py` inlines `report.js` without its JSDoc types (the shared `strip_types`), so annotations never reach the page; `beamdswitch.js` is the site's template, unchanged, pinned by its SHA-256 in the tests.

The prices are synthetic Black-Scholes prices, never market data.

Its tests are in `tests/` and its type check in `tsconfig.json`; `python3 ../../scripts/check.py breeden-litzenberger-density` runs them. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
