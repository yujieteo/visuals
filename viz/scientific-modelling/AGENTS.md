# Scientific Modelling and Dimensional Analysis

`build.py` writes `raw.json` from `data/` and `index.html` from the kit's shell (`scripts/kit/shell.html`), `src/` and `beamdswitch.js`. Never edit either by hand: change the sources, then run `python3 build.py`. The engine is classic-script modules that the tests load with `require()`: `rational`, `linalg`, `units`, `expr`, `sym`, `record`, `check`, `finder`, `nondim`, `special`, `heat`, `asymptotic`, `declare`, `conduction`, `numerics`, `convection`, `radiation`, `structures-num`, `structures`, `flows`, `ode`, `regime`, `stability`, `model`, `stabreport` and `report`. `regimeview.js`, `stabview.js` and `view.js` run only in the browser. A family of declared models is a data entry in `data/catalogue.json` (the six parts of section 10) and a module that `regime.js` lists in `IMPLS` (`conduction.js` is the example; `convection.js` and `radiation.js` of piece 4 also give the stability analysis; `structures.js` of piece 5 holds nine structures models on its own kernel `structures-num.js`, and its stability results reach the panel through the generic branch of `stability.js`, `stabview.js` and `stabreport.js`; `flows.js` of piece 6 holds the six flow models, and its solution panels use the same generic branch with their own heading and derivation). Custom ODE systems go through `ode.js`, and `stability.js` adds the exact symbolic checks of hand calculation 9.

`spec.md` is the captain's specification with the agreed decisions of the build plan at its end; the page is built in nine pieces, and `data/roadmap.json` says which piece brings each family and method. Keep every "exact" status on rational or canonical-form equality (`sym.equal`), never a floating-point comparison. `data/references.json` comes from `tools/references.py` (SymPy and mpmath, pinned with `uv run --with`); run it by hand when an example, its scales or a declared model change, because CI never runs it. The same holds for `data/structures.json` and `tools/structures_references.py` (mpmath, SymPy and SciPy), for `data/flows.json` and `tools/flow_references.py` (SymPy and mpmath), and for the `computed` part of `data/stability.json` and `tools/stability_references.py`; its `wgd` part is a transcription of Table 1S of Wen, Goluskin and Doering (2022). A numerical status always states its tolerance. Write reader text in ASD-STE100.

Its tests are in `tests/` and `e2e/`. `python3 ../../scripts/check.py scientific-modelling` runs its checks. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
