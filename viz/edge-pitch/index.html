Fastener edge margin and pitch visualiser

Explore how end distance, side edge distance, pitch and row spacing drive the margins of a riveted or bolted sheet joint. Strength margins (bearing, shear-out, net section, side edge, inter-rivet buckling) and geometric margins (e/D, p/D) are reported separately, against the loads and allowables you enter.

The tool ships with no allowables. The starting numbers are round placeholders, tagged unsourced default, not material data. Items tagged Niu are marked "confirm against your copy"; no Niu table or figure is reproduced.
