# Étale Fundamental Group

From loops to covers to Galois symmetry. A geometric laboratory for the étale
fundamental group that follows Tamás Szamuely, *Galois Groups and Fundamental
Groups* (Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 117). Every computation is drawn
before it is written: the curve, its punctures, a loop, a finite cover sheet by
sheet, one fibre, the permutation the loop induces, the finite quotient, and the
inverse limit of all of them.

`index.html` is one self-contained file with no network requests (a
Content-Security-Policy forbids them). It works from `file://` and offline, has
light and dark themes (following the system, or chosen with the Theme button),
respects reduced motion (animations jump to their end state), and keeps the
dictionary, the Szamuely concept map and the results table when JavaScript is off.

## What is computed, not drawn by hand

- **Monodromy.** Loops are lifted through the covering equations by Newton
  continuation (`liftPath`). The loop round 𝔾ₘ lifted through xⁿ = t gives the
  n-cycle; loops round 0, 1 and ∞ lifted through the Chebyshev cover
  x ↦ (1 − T₃(x))/2 give two transpositions and a 3-cycle whose product is the
  identity, which is also the dessin teaser.
- **Finite covers of the pair of pants.** The S₃ example a ↦ (12), b ↦ (123) is
  realised as the regular action on the six elements of S₃ (a transitive
  Schreier graph); deck groups are computed as centralisers.
- **Surfaces.** Presentations, Euler characteristics, the elimination of the last
  puncture loop (free rank 2g + r − 1), and the 4g-gon gluing, whose corners are
  tracked to a single vertex.
- **Tori.** E[n] as (1/n)Λ/Λ with n² points; A[n] with n²ᵍ points.
- **Characteristic p.** Frobenius necklaces are orbits of α in 𝔽ₚ[α]/(f) for the
  first irreducible f (Rabin's test). Artin–Schreier fibres are the fields
  𝔽ₚ[y]/(yᵖ − y − t), on which Frobenius is y ↦ y + t. Ordinary versus
  supersingular comes from the Hasse invariant, cross-checked against the point
  count (Hasse invariant ≡ aₚ mod p).
- **Arithmetic π₁.** σₐ : ζ ↦ ζᵃ on μₙ, with σₐ⁻¹γσₐ = γᵃ checked.

The page reruns a check for every row of the results table when it loads and
marks it ✓.

## Scope and honesty

- Results are stated with the assumptions of the specification's computation set
  (for example ℙ¹, 𝔸¹ and 𝔾ₘ over an algebraically closed field of
  characteristic 0).
- π₁ᵉᵗ(𝔸¹) in characteristic p is not finitely generated; the page shows its
  Artin–Schreier ℤ/p quotients only.
- An ordinary elliptic curve in characteristic p has étale Tₚ of rank 1 (ℤₚ), a
  supersingular one rank 0; prime-to-p Tate modules have rank 2.
- Each picture is labelled as a literal complex picture, an algebraic schematic,
  or a topological model (analogy). Characteristic-p pictures never pretend to be
  complex surfaces; abelian varieties of dimension g > 1 are drawn as products of
  tori, not as embedded surfaces.
- Szamuely is cited by chapter only, with the chapter titles as published.

## Tests

`tests/etale-fundamental-group.test.mjs` (Node's built-in runner) checks that the
built files are current, the mathematics above, the results table against the
specification, the chapter-level citations, the self-contained page, the WebMCP
tools against the catalogue stub, and the beamdswitch and Copy deck buttons with
beamdswitch's own deck parser. `tests/beamdswitch-deck-checks.mjs` and the
read-only copies in `tests/fixtures/beamdswitch/` support the deck checks. From the
repository root:

```sh
node --test 'tests/*.test.mjs'
node build.mjs --check
```
