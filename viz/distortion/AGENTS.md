# Structural Distortion Explorer

`index.html` is generated by `build.mjs` (`node build.mjs --check` checks it) from `template.html`, `kinematics.js` (the pure deformation model and beamdswitch report: no DOM, no three.js) and the checked-in three.js bundle `vendor/three.min.js`; [README.md](README.md) says how that bundle is made. Never edit `index.html` by hand.

Label every effect as analytic or an assumed shape, and never add units or calibrated numbers: the view is qualitative and not to scale.

`beamdswitch.js` is the site's template, pinned by its SHA-256 in the tests.

Its tests are in `tests/`; `python3 ../../scripts/check.py distortion` runs its checks. Rules for every visual: [SKILLS.md](../../SKILLS.md).
